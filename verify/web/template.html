Record check

Drop a writing record here, with its manuscript if you have it. Everything is checked in this page. Nothing you drop leaves your computer: this page cannot make a network request.

    This check reports what holds and what doesn’t. It gives no verdict and no score. A record shows what a writing app wrote down, and that it existed by the times shown. It does not show who wrote a text, or that a person composed it.

    Verifier . Works offline: save this page and open it from your disk.
